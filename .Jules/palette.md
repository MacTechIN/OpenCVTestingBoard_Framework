## 2024-07-27 - Accessible Icon Buttons over Images
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons positioned absolutely over images (like a "remove" or "close" button in an overflow-hidden container) often lack sufficient accessible names and their native focus rings can be clipped, making keyboard navigation difficult to perceive.
**Action:** Always add an explicit `aria-label` utilizing translations (e.g., `t('removeImage')`) to the button, set `aria-hidden="true"` on the internal SVG/icon to prevent redundant screen reader announcements, and apply Tailwind `:focus-visible` utility classes with `ring-offset` and `outline-none` (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-red-500`) to ensure a visible focus indicator that works well visually against image backgrounds without being clipped.
