## 2024-09-03 - Focus Ring Clipping on Absolute Positioned Buttons
**Learning:** In the `ImageUploader` component, the absolute positioned "remove image" button is nested within a relative container that has `overflow-hidden` applied to an adjacent sibling (the image container). When applying standard `focus-visible:ring` to the button, the ring is susceptible to clipping if it goes out of bounds, though `shadow-lg` helps slightly. Moreover, applying intent-specific focus ring colors (like `ring-red-500` for destructive actions) greatly enhances the semantic meaning of the focus state. Adding `aria-hidden="true"` on the SVG icon is also necessary to keep the accessibility tree clean for icon-only buttons.
**Action:** Always test the visibility of focus rings on absolutely positioned elements, especially inside or near `overflow-hidden` containers. Use `focus-visible:ring-offset-2` alongside the ring to create necessary space. For destructive actions, use `focus-visible:ring-red-500` to indicate the intent. Always add `aria-label` to the button and `aria-hidden="true"` to the inner icon element.
