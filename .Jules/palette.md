## 2024-03-20 - [A11y & UX] Icon-only Overlay Buttons Focus Rings & ARIA
**Learning:** Absolutely positioned icon-only buttons (like image removal overlays) often lack proper screen reader context and visible focus states. Furthermore, focus rings on absolute positioned elements within overflow-hidden containers can clip or be invisible.
**Action:** Always replace basic `title` attributes with explicit `aria-label` using translation functions for context. Add `aria-hidden="true"` to the internal decorative icon. To ensure visible keyboard navigation without clipping, apply `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2` (customized to semantic intent, e.g., `ring-red-500` for delete) combined with `shadow-lg` to separate the element from its background visually.
