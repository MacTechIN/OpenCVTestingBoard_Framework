## 2024-08-12 - Accessibility and focus rings for absolute positioned elements
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like a close button over an image) require explicit `aria-label`s and `aria-hidden="true"` on their internal icons for screen readers. Furthermore, when adding focus rings to absolute positioned buttons inside or overlapping `overflow-hidden` containers, standard focus styles can get clipped or look awkward.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` (using translation functions if available) to icon-only buttons. Apply `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-none` and coordinate the ring color with the action (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-red-500` for destructive actions like deleting/closing). Use `shadow-lg` to help the button and ring stand out against complex image backgrounds and avoid clipping issues.
