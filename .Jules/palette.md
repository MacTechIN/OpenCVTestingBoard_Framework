## 2023-10-24 - [Accessible Focus Rings on Floating Elements]
**Learning:** When adding focus states to absolutely positioned icon buttons (like the close/remove image button) within an `overflow-hidden` or constrained container, standard `focus` or simple `outline` styles often get clipped or visually blend poorly with the background image. Adding `shadow-lg` combined with `focus-visible:ring-offset-2` provides necessary visual separation, while mapping the ring color to the action's intent (`focus-visible:ring-red-500` for destructive, `focus-visible:ring-blue-500` otherwise) improves semantic accessibility.
**Action:** Always combine `ring-offset` and `shadow` when styling focus states for absolute positioned overlay buttons, and ensure the ring color matches the button's intended semantic meaning.
