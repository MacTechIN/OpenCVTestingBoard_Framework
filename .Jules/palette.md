## 2024-07-28 - Focus Rings on Absolute Positioned Icon Buttons
**Learning:** In the `ImageUploader` component, the absolute-positioned "Remove Image" icon button within the `overflow-hidden` container required `focus-visible:ring-offset-2` alongside the `focus-visible:ring-2` to prevent the focus ring from appearing clipped or invisible due to the container's overflow constraints. Additionally, applying shadow (`shadow-lg`) helps visually separate the ring from the underlying image.
**Action:** When adding focus states to absolute-positioned buttons over images in `overflow-hidden` containers, always use `ring-offset-2` (and potentially a shadow) to ensure the focus ring remains fully visible and distinct. Adapt the ring color to match the intent (e.g., `ring-red-500` for destructive actions).
