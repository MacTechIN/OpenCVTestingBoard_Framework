## 2024-05-19 - Accessible absolute-positioned overlay buttons
**Learning:** Absolute-positioned overlay buttons (e.g., the 'remove image' cross over an image inside an `overflow-hidden` container) can easily have their focus rings clipped if not styled properly. Icon-only buttons also consistently need explicit `aria-label`s and `aria-hidden="true"` on the SVG itself to prevent redundant or confusing screen reader announcements.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` to icon-only buttons, set `aria-hidden="true"` on the internal icon, and when the button is positioned absolutely over content, apply `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-none` and `shadow-lg` to ensure the focus ring remains clearly visible and isn't clipped. Adapt the ring color to match the button's intent (e.g., red for destructive).
