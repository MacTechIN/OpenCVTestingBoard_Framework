## 2025-05-10 - Explicit Accessibility in Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** In this project's custom component architecture, icon-only utility buttons (like the `<X />` to clear uploaded images in `ImageUploader`) heavily rely on visual presentation without standard `aria-label`s or focus rings that meet accessibility guidelines out-of-the-box. Screen readers may misinterpret or completely omit these buttons unless properties are explicitly defined.
**Action:** When working on interactive UI elements going forward, always verify that icon-only buttons have an explicit `aria-label` utilizing the `t()` translation function, use `aria-hidden="true"` on the internal SVG/icon elements, and apply robust `:focus-visible` styling (like `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`) from Tailwind to ensure proper keyboard navigation.
