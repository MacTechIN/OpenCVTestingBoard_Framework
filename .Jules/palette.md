## 2024-08-18 - [Accessible Overlay Icon Buttons]
**Learning:** For absolute positioned overlay buttons (e.g., a remove/close button over an image) within `overflow-hidden` containers, standard focus rings are often clipped or completely hidden. Additionally, icon-only buttons need explicit `aria-label` utilizing translation functions and `aria-hidden="true"` on the SVG to prevent redundant or missing screen reader announcements.
**Action:** Always combine `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-none` with `shadow-lg` to ensure the focus ring is not clipped. Adapt the ring color to the action (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-red-500` for destructive actions). Ensure icon-only buttons always have an `aria-label` and `aria-hidden="true"` on their child icons.
