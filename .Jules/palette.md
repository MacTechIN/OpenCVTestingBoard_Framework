## 2024-05-14 - Icon-only Remove Image Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Absolute positioned overlay icon-only buttons (like a remove 'X' button on top of an image) often lack explicit semantic labels and proper focus visibility. They can be overlooked by keyboard users or screen readers. Also, when placed in an `overflow-hidden` container, `focus-visible` rings might get clipped unless handled carefully with shadow-lg or proper padding.
**Action:** When implementing absolute positioned, icon-only overlay actions, always add an `aria-label` translating the intent (e.g. `t('removeImage')`), hide the inner icon (`aria-hidden="true"`), and provide custom `focus-visible` ring classes (like `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-red-500`) to guarantee keyboard focus is distinguishable.
