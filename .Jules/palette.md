## 2026-02-14 - Improve accessibility of ImageUploader buttons
**Learning:** Focus rings on absolute positioned overlay buttons (e.g., over images) within `overflow-hidden` containers can be clipped or invisible, hindering keyboard accessibility. Icon-only buttons also require explicit `aria-label` attributes.
**Action:** When designing overlay buttons within `overflow-hidden` containers, add `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-none` combined with `shadow-lg` to prevent the focus ring from clipping. Ensure all icon-only buttons include an `aria-label` attribute and `aria-hidden="true"` on the internal icon element. Adapt the ring color to the action's intent (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-red-500` for destructive actions, `focus-visible:ring-blue-500` otherwise).
